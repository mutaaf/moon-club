# Moon Club 0.12.0: "Party Moon"

Moon Club learned to throw a party. Every right answer sparkles, a combo makes it rain confetti, and beating a boss sets off fireworks big enough to make Mr. Fredricksen's balloon house jealous. Luna talks now too, and on iPhone Duo the hinge is part of the game.

## Luna's news

- [book.pages.fill] On iPhone Duo, fold me like a book, set me down like a laptop, or open wide to launch!
- [sparkles] A Mystery Stop sparkles on the map each day. Find it for bonus points!
- [star.square.on.square.fill] New sticker book: every stop you win is a sticker. Three stars make it shiny.

## What to test
- **Three sizes of party.** A right answer pops sparkles from the button you tapped, coins fly to the top and Luna hops. A combo of 3 (or 6), a perfect round or a new badge tier gives confetti, a glow round the screen and a Luna flip. Beating a boss, winning a land, a new rank, a mission chest or a 3, 7 or 30 day streak gets fireworks, two confetti cannons, a little screen shake and a rising chime. Reduce Motion swaps it all for a soft glow and a tap.
- **Luna talks.** A little bubble pops out of Luna in the game bar: on your first win of the day, when you bounce back after a miss, on a combo, and now and then after a miss. Short, kind, never pushy.
- **Liquid Glass.** On iOS 26 and later, cards, the game bar, answer buttons, badges, the shop and toasts are made of Liquid Glass, and the points pill flows into the results card when a game ends. Tapping a map stop zooms into its game, like stepping through the Stargate. Older iOS keeps the frosted look; Reduce Transparency makes everything solid.
- **Mystery Stop.** One open chart game a day wears a gold MYSTERY tag. Finish it today for 15 bonus points. It's the same stop on all your devices.
- **Almost there.** The results screen says what's close: two more stars for perfect, one stop left to win a land, three more for silver.
- **Sticker book.** A new Stickers tab in the Moon Locker. Every stop and land is a sticker; three stars make it shiny.
- **Goodnight.** After about 20 minutes, between games, Luna gets sleepy and offers a goodnight. "One more game" is always fine. Never in the middle of a game.
- **iPhone Duo (only on iPhone Duo; nothing changes elsewhere):**
  - A first-open coach: "Fold me like a book!" shows the three poses.
  - **Book:** lessons are two-page storybooks; the badge shelf and sticker book sit on facing pages.
  - **Tabletop:** in a Chart Time Machine boss the chart stands up on the top half and the clues become a game pad on the bottom.
  - **The hinge as a control:** in Build the Candle, open or close the phone to slide a price up and down. A ready chest opens when you open the phone wide, and a new rank waits behind "Open me wide!".
  - **Game Night:** two even halves; lying flat, the top player's half turns to face them across the table. The final score gets fireworks.
  - Three iPhone Duo badges (Storybook Reader, Tabletop Trader, Hinge Hero) and a Storybook frame in the shop. A "Made for iPhone Duo" tag shows wherever one of these is on.

## Our promises
Still no ads, no purchases, nothing about your kid leaves the device. The goodnight never takes anything away, and the Mystery Stop never counts down. To infinity and beyond, but only after a good night's sleep.
