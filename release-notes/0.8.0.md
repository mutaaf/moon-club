# Moon Club 0.8.0: "Previously on Moon Club…"

Every great show starts with a recap, and now Moon Club does too. After each update, Luna pops up with a quick "previously on" reel of what's new, like the opening of every Star Wars movie, except the text doesn't fly away into space.

## Luna's news
- [gift.fill] After each update, I'll show you what's new, just like this!
- [hand.draw.fill] Swipe through the cards, then tap Let's go.

## What to test
- **The update reel.** Install this build over an older one. Luna appears with a burst of stars and one card per new thing. Swipe or tap Next, then Let's go. It should show once, then never again for this version. It's the Groundhog Day of modals, but it only happens one time.
- **Missed a few updates?** If you skipped versions, the reel stacks them, newest first, up to five cards. Like a Netflix "previously on", with no cliffhangers.
- **Skip.** Tap Skip in the corner and Luna takes the hint, like a polite Jedi.
- **A brand-new install shows nothing.** Everything is new anyway, so there's no reel.
- **Grown-ups:** the full notes, pop culture and all, are still under For grown-ups → What's new.

## Our promises
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. The "last seen version" lives only on this device.

Found a bug? Screenshot it in TestFlight. That's what Mission Control is for.
