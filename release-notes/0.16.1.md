# Moon Club 0.16.1: "Start Here"

Our testers told us the truth: coming back to the app felt like opening every door at Monsters, Inc. at once. Now Luna says hi and points to one big bright button. Tap it and you're off. Everything else waits quietly until you want it.

## Luna's news
- [hand.point.up.left.fill] Not sure where to start? Tap the big bright button. I picked your next step!
- [checklist] Your other missions wait in a small list below.

## What to test
- **Story tab, first thing.** Luna says "Welcome back! Next up:" (or "Hi! Let's start here." the very first time) above ONE big glowing button with the next step and how long it takes, like "Learn: What is a report card? · 2 min". It's the only bright button on the screen. Can a kid tell what to tap in two seconds? That's the whole test, as Dory would say: just keep swimming.
- **How it picks.** A half-done quest chapter first ("Keep going"), then the first unfinished daily mission, then the mission chest, then the next World stop (the World tab gets a small glowing dot), then Guess or the Piggy Bank.
- **The rest.** Other missions are small chips under "Then", with dots for 1/3 and the streak. The chest is a little ring that fills up. The date, weekly recap and story cards fold into "Today's story".
- **iPad.** The big button sits on the left; missions and the story on the right.

## Our promises
Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and everything stays on the device. Pretend money only, never advice. To infinity and beyond, one button at a time.
