# Moon Club 0.13.0: "Moon Garden"

Moon points grow now. Plant some in a company you know, and they grow or shrink with its real price, one calm day at a time. It's patient work, like WALL-E looking after his one little plant. And the iPad got a proper glow-up: no more cards running off the edge.

## What to test
- **Moon Garden (Piggy Bank tab, "Moon Garden" side).** Tap Plant points, pick a company, slide how many, and say yes to "Plant 40 points in Disney?". The points leave your spending pile and start growing. Like Groot, they take their time.
  - **Prices change once a day** (the last closing price), never live. The card says when the market is sleeping.
  - **Tap a planting** to see its line since you planted, why the price moved (the same "Why did it move?" clues as the Story), and a slider to harvest some or all of it back. Gains come back as points to spend; a dip is a small, real loss.
  - **Guardrails.** You can plant up to half your points each day, so your Moon Shop points never vanish in one go. Grown-ups can change it (or pause planting) in the grown-up corner, under Moon Garden. No borrowing, no tiny plantings, no tricks.
  - **Habit cards.** "All your points are in one basket" when one company holds most of it, and a daily "check less often" card. No notifications, ever.
  - **My points** (tap the rank pill, then My points) shows the whole sum: earned, plus or minus harvests, minus the shop, minus what's growing, equals what you can spend.
  - **Two new badges** in the cabinet: Spread it out (3, 5, 8 companies at once) and Patient planter (7, 30, 90 days).
  - **Two devices?** Plantings and harvests sync like a set of Lego bricks: each one counts once, even if both devices harvest the same planting offline.
- **iPad.** The Story is a grid of cards on a wide screen instead of a carousel that ran off the edge. Big names no longer break mid-word. The World map gets most of the room and its header stacks instead of cutting off. Try portrait and landscape, with the sidebar shown and hidden, and in Split View and Stage Manager.
- **Smoother.** Points, ranks and badges are worked out once per change instead of once per view; boss backgrounds and the aurora run at a calm frame rate and rest when nobody can see them.
- **Colour-blind friendly candles.** Up candles are hollow and down candles are filled (big candles get an arrow too), so you can read a chart in black and white.
- **Read the Day.** The Start and Low labels no longer sit on top of each other when they're the same price.

## Our promises
Moon points are pretend. They aren't money, they can't be cashed out, and nothing can be bought. Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and everything stays on the device. Patience you must have, my young planter.
