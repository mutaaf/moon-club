# Moon Club 0.19.0: "Chart Academy"

The candle games were the hit of the tower, so we built a whole school for them. The purple button on the World map is now the **Moon Arcade**, with three climbs inside: Candle Academy, Line Lab and Moon Tower.

**Candle Academy: 300 levels.** 39 real candlestick patterns, from the big up candle and the doji to hammers, shooting stars, swallows (engulfing), babies (harami), morning and evening stars, three marchers and the rare lonely baby. A market technician wrote the exact rules for every pattern, and a teacher shaped how they're taught: watch, then finish, then do it alone, with old patterns coming back so they stick. Ten kinds of drill: Who won the tug-of-war, Candle parts, Story to candle, Build it, Fix the candle, Name it, Spot it (a hidden-picture hunt worthy of Where's Waldo), Odd one out, What might happen and Catch it (like Snap). Every chapter ends with a boss, and Pattern Detective at the end mixes everything, Sherlock style.

**Line Lab: 220 levels.** The next step after candles: drag two dots to draw a trend line, slide a floor or ceiling into place, line up both rails of a lane, find the breakout and see if it stuck, make a moving average yourself from the last few days, spot where the fast average crosses the slow one, find the biggest crowd on the volume bars, and learn liquidity with pretend order books (the gap between buyers and sellers, and why a big order might slip in a quiet market). It ends with The Whole Picture: trend, floor, average and crowd together. Wax on, wax off: practice is the whole plan.

**Exact on every screen.** Lines are graded in days and prices, not in screen points, so a line that passes on an iPhone SE passes on an iPad or the open iPhone Duo. Handles are big, they have a gentle magnet that pulls them onto a wick tip low in the lab (it switches off near the top), and after every try the exact line draws in gold next to yours.

**iPhone Duo.** Held wide, the chart sits on one side and the question on the other. In tabletop, the chart is up top and the bottom half is your game pad. Half open, the hinge is a knob: fold to tilt your trend line, slide a floor, move the average dot or set a candle's high.

**Moving pictures in lessons.** Every picture in Moon School now moves, not just the first one in each lesson. They animate briefly, then settle into a cached still image, so the battery and the frame rate stay happy.

Charts give clues, not promises: "What might happen next?" is scored on reading the clue, and then you see what really happened. Sometimes it goes the other way, just like the real thing.

## What to test
- **Open it:** World tab → the purple Arcade button → Candle Academy or Line Lab. The path should open on your chapter.
- **Candle Academy:** levels 1–3 teach with a tip, then you're on your own. Try level 7 (Build it), 9 (Candle parts), 26 (Odd one out), 46 (Spot it), 74 (Catch it), 88 (Fix the candle).
- **Line Lab:** level 21 (draw a trend line), 61 (floor), 101 (lane), 141 (make the average), 161 (find the cross), 189 (busy or quiet market). Is the line grading fair? Too strict or too loose?
- **iPhone Duo:** hold it open sideways for the side-by-side layout; half open, fold to move the picked dot.
- **Lessons:** open any lesson and step past the first page: the pictures should move, then rest.
