# Moon Club 0.17.2: "One Big Page"

Found it for real this time. With an iPhone Duo open, the Story tab kept rearranging itself behind whatever you had just started, over and over, so the new screen never got a turn. Like Doctor Strange stuck in a time loop, it couldn't stop on its own. Now it can't: the open Duo is one big page again, and screens that slide out of sight hold still.

## What to test
- **iPhone Duo open, start anything.** Tap Play on the big card, then open a lesson, a game, a quest and a boss from Story, World and Guess. Each should open right away and keep moving.
- **The crease.** Open flat or like a book: the colors and words run straight across, with no dark band down the middle.
- **Closed Duo, iPhone, iPad.** Should look and play like 0.17.1.
- **If anything freezes:** wait for it to close, then tap **Share** when TestFlight asks. Like Ghostbusters, we know who to call.
