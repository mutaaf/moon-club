# Moon Club 0.2.0: "I am Iron Moon" 🦾🌙

Luna got her Iron Man suit upgrade. Grown-ups now pick her brain, literally: three options, one tap each, and the same kid-safe rules in every suit.

## What to test
- **Luna's brain picker.** For grown-ups → Luna's brain. Three cards, plain words, no settings maze. Like choosing a starter Pokémon, except all three are friendly.
- **On this device (Apple Intelligence).** Luna thinks right on the iPhone or iPad, and nothing leaves the device. It's the default when your device supports it. If it doesn't, the card says why and how to fix it, Yoda-calm: "turn it on, you must."
- **Free Luna.** The same free helper as before. Still the trusty sidekick, Chewie to everyone's Han.
- **Your own key = Super Luna.** Paste an Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or xAI key, then tap check (it reports back with the model it picked) and flip the OK switch. You get longer answers, follow-up memory and a nicer voice. Remove it in one tap, faster than a Mario Kart blue shell.
- **Follow-up memory.** On-device and Super Luna remember the chat, so "why?" after "what's a share?" just works. Tap New chat to start fresh, Groundhog Day style.
- **Explain more.** On story, weather and word cards, ask Luna to go deeper. Bonus round, Jeopardy music optional.
- **The brain badge.** Ask Luna shows who's thinking: a moon with sparkles for on-device, a key for Super Luna.

## Grown-ups
- With your own key, your child's questions go straight to the company you picked, and only after you flip the OK switch. Change company and the switch resets.
- The key lives in the Keychain. Use one with a spending limit.
- Every answer is checked on the device before your child sees it. Advice, off-topic or unsafe replies become a gentle "ask a grown-up".

## Our promises
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Progress never leaves the device, and Luna never asks who you are.
