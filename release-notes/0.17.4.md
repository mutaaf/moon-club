# Moon Club 0.17.4: "Radio Dial"

The iPhone Duo's hinge is now a slider, too. In the Busy day? game in Signal Sea, hold the phone half open and open or close it a little: a glow slides across the 20 days, with a tiny tick on each one, like tuning an old radio in Stranger Things. Find the tallest volume bar, then tap "Pick this day". Tapping a bar still works on every phone and iPad.
