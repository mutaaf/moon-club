# Moon Club 0.17.3: "Flip It"

The iPhone Duo's hinge is now a page turner. Give the fold a quick flick (close it a little and open it back) and the page turns: in a lesson it does what the big button does, in Today's story it turns the card, and in the World it turns the atlas. Like Marty McFly's hoverboard, no hands needed.

Guesses get an envelope. On an open iPhone Duo, tap Beat or Miss and your pick waits in an envelope whose flap follows the hinge; close the phone and it's sealed and sent. Tapping "Lock it in" works too, and phones and iPads still save with one tap, the way a Hogwarts letter only counts once it's sealed.

Tent mode no longer pops up on its own. Holding the phone half open in two hands looked like a tent and started the puppet show. Now the puppet show is a button you choose: stand the phone up like an open book facing your family.

## What to test
- **iPhone Duo, half open:** in a lesson, flick the fold (close it about a quarter and open it back, quickly). The lesson moves on, the same as tapping Next. It never skips a quiz you haven't answered.
- **Today's story:** open it on Story, then flick. The card turns.
- **Guess, Duo open:** tap Beat or Miss. An envelope appears (tap the same pick again to take it back). Close the phone: the guess shows under "Your guesses" when you open it. "Lock it in" does the same without closing.
- **Holding it half open** for a while should not start the puppet show any more. The Puppet show button still works.
- **Closed Duo, iPhone, iPad:** same as 0.17.2.

## Our promises
Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and no camera. Everything stays on the device. Pretend money only, never advice. Like Mary Poppins with a spoonful of sugar, a little flick makes the lesson go down.
