# Moon Club 0.9.1: "Spit and polish, Captain"

A quick pit stop, Mario Kart style: new tires, shiny paint, same rocket. Nothing big, just everything sitting where it should.

## Luna's news
- [sparkles] I tidied up the map, so nothing sits on top of anything now!
- [wrench.and.screwdriver.fill] I fixed the Piggy Bank, so it never closes the app by surprise.

## What to test
- **The Piggy Bank crash is gone.** In 0.8.0, Moon Club could crash if the piggy bank was cleared while you were looking at it. That bug has been sent to the Phantom Zone. Open Piggy Bank, tap Start over, and it should calmly take you back to setup.
- **Badges.** New badges now pop up as a small pill just above the tab bar. They never cover a title, they leave by themselves after a few seconds, and each badge shows only once. Like a Pokémon level-up jingle: short, happy, gone.
- **The map.** Stops have room to breathe, and the stars never touch the next stop. Luna's rocket now parks beside the stop she's on, like the Millennium Falcon docked next to the station instead of on top of it.
- **Build the candle.** The handles start spread out in their own lanes (Start on the left, End on the right, High and Low on the wick). A faint ghost candle shows what you're building, and the labels never bump into each other.
- **Story recap.** The weekly recap card is shorter, and its Read-it-to-me button sits right next to the title, so it's never hiding behind the tab bar.
