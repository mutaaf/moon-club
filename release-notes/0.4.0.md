# Moon Club 0.4.0: "Luna has her own Bat-Signal" 🦇🌙

Luna can now hang out outside the app: on your Home Screen, your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Think of it as Mission Control for the Moon Club, minus the Apollo 13 drama.

## What to test
- **Report Card Day widget.** Add it from the widget gallery (long-press the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search Moon Club). Tap Beat 👍 or Miss 👎 right on the widget. It should switch the moment your finger lifts. No app launch, no loading spinner, faster than the Flash ordering a smoothie.
- **Market Weather widget.** Today's market mood as weather. On the medium size, tap "I read it" for a little checkmark of glory.
- **Pretend Piggy Bank widget.** Your pretend coins with a tiny sparkline. Like Scrooge McDuck's vault, but it fits in your pocket and nothing is real.
- **Moon School widget.** Your lesson streak and the next one-minute lesson. Tap Start and it opens that exact lesson. Wax on, wax off.
- **Luna's Moon widget.** Tonight's real moon phase, drawn as Luna. Go outside, look up and check her work, like a tiny NASA intern.
- **Lock Screen and StandBy.** Add the small circle and rectangle widgets to your Lock Screen. Put your phone on its side while charging to see StandBy.
- **Live Activity: Report Card Day.** Guess a company that reports today, then lock your phone. A countdown ticks on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island until the report card comes out, then it flips to "Results are in!" like a Pixar twist ending.
- **Live Activity: Lesson in progress.** Start a Moon School lesson, then swipe home. The Dynamic Island keeps your place with a little moon ring. Tap it to jump back in.
- **Dark mode and tinted icons.** Flip them on. Everything should still read clearly, like Batman in the Batcave.

## Our promises
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Widgets read a tiny file on this device and never touch the internet. Live Activities update right here on the phone, with no push servers. What happens in Moon Club stays on the device.

Found a bug? Screenshot it in TestFlight. Even Luke needed a few tries to bullseye womp rats.

To the Lock Screen, and beyond! 🚀
