# Moon Club 0.14.0: "Moonlight"

Moon Club learned to paint with light. Market World has real water now, fog that burns away like a dragon's breath when you win a land, and Luna got a proper moon face with craters and a glow. And on iPhone Duo, the hinge is a light switch, a page turner, a theater curtain and a race track. Great Scott, that's a lot of folding.

## Luna's news
- [water.waves] Market World sparkles now: shiny water, fog that burns away and a glowing trail!
- [moon.fill] I got a glow-up too. Look at my craters!
- [theatermasks.fill] On iPhone Duo: stand me up for a puppet show, or lay me flat for a Market Race!
- [book.pages.fill] Open your iPhone Duo like a book and the World turns into an atlas.

## What to test
- **Market World (World tab).** The sea is drawn on the graphics chip: caustic light, gentle swells, and each land's water tinted its own color. Scroll and the water moves with the islands. Locked lands sit under swirling fog; win a land (or launch with the debug "conquer" option) and the fog burns away with golden embers, like Smaug's breath but friendly. When Luna's rocket flies to the next stop, a comet of light runs along the trail. Tilt the phone a tiny bit: the clouds float on their own layer.
- **Luna.** Every Luna bigger than a thumbnail is a shaded little moon now (craters, rim light, a soft glow). Tiny Lunas stay simple and fast.
- **Charts.** Get a candle right and the chart blooms. The Chart Time Machine bosses travel forward in time with a sci-fi color sweep and film grain, like a DeLorean hitting 88. Beat any boss and the screen ripples with a shockwave. A planting in the Moon Garden now has a whole year of candles to swipe through.
- **Parties.** Confetti, fireworks and sparkles are now GPU particles: more of them, smoother, and they cost nothing when the party's over.
- **Shiny stuff.** Gold badges and rare Moon Shop items shimmer; iPhone Duo items tilt like a hologram card.
- **iPhone Duo**, every pose has a job:
  - **Closed:** Luna's pocket on the little screen. She naps until you pick the phone up, then shows today's missions and your garden.
  - **Book:** the World becomes an atlas. Left page is the land's story, right page is the island. Swipe the page or give the fold a quick flick to turn it.
  - **Tent** (stand it up, half open): Luna's puppet show of today's story, facing the family, with the controls on your side. The puppet show can play on a TV too.
  - **Flat:** Game Night 2.0, the Market Race. One half each, both rockets race along a track on the fold. First right answer gets a boost.
  - **The hinge itself:** open slowly and the sun rises over the sea, Luna's light swings round, the fog over locked lands parts a little, and the crease gets a soft shadow. Close the phone while planting in the Moon Garden and the pot's lid shuts: planted. That's "fold to save".
  - **New iPhone Duo badges:** Puppet Master, Market Racer, Atlas Explorer and Fold Saver, plus a Twin Moon rocket and a Twin moon halo for Luna in the Moon Shop. The Duo tour in What's New shows every pose.
- **Battery and speed.** Every effect rests when you can't see it, in Low Power Mode, and when the phone gets warm. Reduce Motion shows calm still frames. iPhone animations can run at 120 frames a second now.

## Our promises
Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and everything stays on the device. The tilt sensor is only read to move clouds and wake Luna; it's never saved or sent. To infinity and beyond, one fold at a time.
