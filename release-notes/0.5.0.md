# Moon Club 0.5.0: "It's dangerous to go alone. Take your progress." 🗺️

Freaky Friday, but for iPads: your progress can now swap into any device you own, and nobody wakes up in the wrong body. Finish a lesson on the iPad, pick up the next one on the iPhone. Like a Portal gun for piggy banks, minus the cake.

## What to test
- **Sync with iCloud.** For grown-ups, then Your devices. Turn it on with two devices on the same Apple ID. Finish a Moon School lesson on one and watch it show up on the other (give iCloud a minute; it's not a TARDIS). Make a guess offline on both, reconnect, and check that every guess is there, with no doubles and no lost points.
- **Points don't double.** Sync is not a duplication glitch, Mario fans. Your level after syncing should match what you actually did, not twice that.
- **Reset, two ways.** Reset all progress now asks: this device only, or all devices. "All devices" is a Thanos snap across every device on that iCloud. "This device only" turns sync off here, so the other devices keep everything.
- **Move my progress from the website.** On the Metaphases Kids website, open the grown-up corner, tap Make my progress code, copy, then paste it in Moon Club under From the website. Paste it twice: nothing doubles. Change a letter: Luna spots it like Sherlock.
- **Your own key on your other devices.** In Luna's brain, after setting up your own key, turn on "Use on my other devices". The key and your OK travel by iCloud Keychain. Switching AI company still asks for a fresh OK.

## Good to know
- Sync is off until a grown-up turns it on, one device at a time. Two kids sharing one Apple ID? Keep it off, or the Weasley twins will share one report card.
- It goes through your own iCloud. We never see it.

## Our promises
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Progress lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, nowhere else.

To infinity and beyond, on every screen in the house. 🌙
