## What to test on iPhone Duo
- **Closed (outer display).** The tab bar slides to the side like a Jedi's lightsaber on a belt. The 🔒 grown-ups button is pinned there and never wanders off.
- **Open it slowly and watch Luna.** On every tab, Luna becomes a moon that fills in as the phone opens: new moon, crescent, half, full. Phases of the moon, one hinge at a time.
- **Piggy Bank, opening.** The pig's coin hatch lifts and coins pop out like a Mario question block. Money on one page, what's inside on the other.
- **Moon School, wide open.** Lessons turn into a storybook: the picture on the left, the play on the right, never on the crease. On a quiz, the left page shows what Luna just said, so the answer is right there.
- **Quests and Story.** Picture-book spreads, two Story cards at a time. Hogwarts textbooks, minus the biting.
- **Finish a lesson, then close the phone.** Luna gets tucked in, eyes shut and snoring 💤. Goodnight, moon.
- **Game Night (new, in Guess).** Two players, one phone: open it like a book and each player gets a half. Picks stay secret until both lock in, then the reveal. Hand it over like a Nintendo DS. Plays top-and-bottom when closed, too.
- **Got a TV?** Connect it during Game Night and the big scoreboard can show up there, Mario Kart living-room style.
- **Luna's Word Book.** Tap a word, then "Keep the Word Book open beside me" for a second window next to your quest. Reading, side by side like Toy Story's best buddies.

## Grown-ups
- No new data, no accounts, no camera. Game Night isn't saved anywhere; it's a party game.
- Everything still works the same on phones and iPads without a hinge.
