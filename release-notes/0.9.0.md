# Moon Club 0.9.0: "It's dangerous to go alone. Take this map!"

Moon School packed its backpack and became **Market World**: a whole overworld to explore, Zelda style. Seven lands, one rocket, and Luna at the window. Fog lifts as you go, and every boss you beat plants your flag like a Pokémon gym badge.

## Luna's news
- [map.fill] Explore Market World and conquer Candle Canyon!
- [chart.bar.xaxis] Learn to read candles, trends, floors and busy days by playing.
- [hourglass] Beat a boss in the Chart Time Machine and see what really happened next.
- [sparkles] Everything got a fresh new look, made just for this app.

## What to test
- **The map (World tab).** Indiana Jones would be proud: a red-line-on-the-map journey from Lemonade Coast to Report Card Castle. Pinch to zoom, scroll around, tap a stop to see its stars, then Play. The map opens right where Luna's rocket is waiting.
- **Fog of war.** Lands you haven't reached are under clouds. Win a land's boss and the next one opens, like Moana finding the next island.
- **Candle Canyon.** Build the candle (drag Start, End, High and Low), Read the day on a real chart, and Candle match.
- **Trend Trails.** Swipe up, down or sideways on real charts, then draw a trendline with your finger.
- **Pattern Peaks.** Drag a line to the floor or ceiling, and play Pattern detective. Every pattern is a clue, not a promise.
- **Signal Sea.** Tap the busiest day on the volume bars, and pick out the smooth moving-average line.
- **Bosses.** Captain's Quiz on the first two lands, and the Chart Time Machine everywhere else: read the clues, make a pretend call, then travel forward in time. Stars come from reading clues, never from the call. Great Scott!
- **No more scrolling inside cards.** Lessons, Story cards and games fit the screen. The Story cards are all the same height now, so swiping never jumps.
- **New look.** One set of drawn icons everywhere (no more emoji as pictures), Luna has moods (happy, thinking, cheering, sleepy), and calmer, snappier animations. Widgets match too, and the dark-mode Piggy Bank widget is easier to read.
- **iPhone Duo.** Opened flat, the map takes one page and the level preview the other.

## Our promises
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Pretend points only. The charts are real public prices, and nothing about your child leaves the device. Your stars sync only through your family's own iCloud, if you turn that on.

Found a bug? Screenshot it in TestFlight. Every bug is a hidden heart container we haven't found yet.
