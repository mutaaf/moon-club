# Moon Club 0.15.0: "Money School"

Moon School went back to school. Luna learned 39 new lessons on the money things kids meet every day, from earning and saving goals to borrowing, rising prices, scams and baskets of companies. Like Remy learning to cook, it all starts with small, real examples. A teacher and a markets expert checked every card.

## What to test
- **Moon School (School tab).** 93 lessons in 20 units now (was 54 in 13). New units: Earning money, Saving goals, Smart spending, Staying safe, Prices over time, Borrowing and interest, and Baskets of companies. New lessons show a "New" tag. Report cards gained the calendar, profit for each share, and sales vs profit.
- **"Let's work it out."** Worked examples show the setup, then one line of math per tap of the big button, like Dash revealing a puzzle one clue at a time. Try "Start a business" or "Profit for each share".
- **Tap to grow.** Growth pictures add a year with every tap (or tap the chart). Try "Growth on growth" (growth on growth vs the same $10 a year), "Prices creep up" and "When owing grows". It's the Moon Garden idea with pretend numbers, and it always says growth can shrink too.
- **Full Moons (challenge).** A third, optional track with percents, fees, credit cards and the Rule of 72, tagged "Challenge". These lessons never hold back map stars, mastery or a land's boss quiz. Even Hermione did extra credit only when she wanted to.
- **Market World.** Lemonade Coast and Saver's Isle have new stops. Stops you already have stars on stay open, units you finished keep their stars, and the rocket stays in the first land you haven't conquered.
- **Luna's refresh.** A question missed at a Review stop brings its whole lesson back to the top of Moon School for a replay. Play it and it clears.
- **Learn why.** After a Guess reveal, and on every "Why did it move?" clue, a "Learn it" button opens the lesson that explains it. The Piggy Bank spots when every company is in the same group, and the Moon Garden has its own Garden School.
- **Grown-up corner.** Each unit under "What your child is learning" opens to dinner table talk: questions to ask at home.

## Our promises
Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and everything stays on the device. Pretend money only, never advice. As Doc Brown would say: your future is whatever you make it, so make it a good one.
