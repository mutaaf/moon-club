# Moon Club 0.11.0: "Points That Matter"

Points used to just sit there, like gold coins in a game with no shop. Now every Moon point you earn by learning can turn into something you can see: a new rocket, a hat for Luna, a flag for every land you win. Think Mario Kart unlocks, minus the banana peels.

## Luna's news
- [bag.fill] The Moon Shop is open! Spend Moon points on rockets, hats, flags and colors.
- [rosette] Badges now go bronze, silver and gold. Tap your moon at the top to see them.
- [moon.stars.fill] New moons to reach, all the way to Moon Legend. Each one opens something new.
- [flame.fill] Come back each day for a bonus that grows with your streak.

## What to test
- **One kind of points.** The coins in a game are now Moon points, and they are the same points as your rank. A run banks its points on the results screen (your best run of a game each day counts, so replaying is for fun, not farming). Results show what the whole run earned and a button to the Moon Shop. Pretend dollars stay in the Piggy Bank, separate.
- **The Moon Locker.** Tap the moon pill at the top left. Three parts:
  - **Badges:** a trophy shelf, six to a page, swipe for more. Chart Reader badges for each chart land, Land Conqueror, Boss Slayer, Moon Streak, Report Card Guesser, Super Saver, Curious Cat (questions for Luna), Bookworm and Mission Maker. Each has bronze, silver and gold, a progress bar, and a hint when it's still locked. The website's story badges have their own page.
  - **Moon Shop:** rockets, Luna's hats, map flags, map trails, app colors, Luna frames and streak savers. Some need a rank or a badge first, like a Pokémon gym badge before the next route. Tap one, then "Get it", then "Yes" (no surprise taps). What you get is worn right away.
  - **My points:** where every point came from: lessons, games, bosses, quests, guesses, daily visits, chests and badges.
- **Wearing it.** Luna wears her hat everywhere she shows up, including Ask Luna and the game HUD (where her frame shows too). Your rocket flies on the map, your trail color paints the path you walked, and your flag goes up on every land you win. App colors repaint the buttons.
- **Ranks.** After Full Moon come Supermoon, Blue Moon, Harvest Moon, Eclipse Explorer and Moon Legend. A new rank gets a full-screen moment once your game is over, showing what it unlocked.
- **Daily visits.** Opening Moon Club each day adds 5 points, plus 5 more each day in a row, up to 25. Missed a day? A streak saver from the shop covers one. No streak saver? You simply start a new streak. Nothing is ever taken away.
- **Mission chests** now give 30 Moon points. Rockets you already got from chests stay yours.
- **Family league** shows your rank and badge count, and each family player's Report Card Guesser tier.
- **Sync.** Buying on one device and another thing on a second device keeps both, and each thing is paid for once.

## Our promises
Moon points are earned only. Nothing costs real money, nothing can be bought with real money, and there are no ads. Questions for Luna are only counted, never saved.
