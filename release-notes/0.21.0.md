# Moon Club 0.21.0: "Arcade, front and center"

The Arcade was hiding behind a small purple chip on the World map, like Platform 9¾. Not anymore.

**The Arcade is on the home screen.** Right under Start here: Candle Academy, Line Lab and Moon Tower, each with the level you're on. One tap and you're climbing. "See all" opens the whole Moon Arcade. The World map's chip still works too.

**Fresher builds, more often.** TestFlight now gets a new build up to three times a day whenever something new lands. It's the Pit Crew approach: small changes, quick stops, back on the track.

**Hunting the open-Duo freeze.** Two freezes came from the Arcade on a fully open iPhone Duo when the app went to the background. We're tracking it down; if it happens, just reopen the app, and nothing you won is lost. These aren't the droids you're looking for, but we're still looking.
