# Moon Club 0.20.0: "Moon Club Pass"

Even the Millennium Falcon needs a fuel stop. The Arcade climbs now come with a daily rhythm, and grown-ups can open everything with one small yearly pass.

**Free forever.** Every lesson, the World map, Guess, Piggy Bank, Ask Luna, the first chapter of Candle Academy and Line Lab, and the first floor of Moon Tower. Replaying any level you've already won is always free, and out of hearts still just means "try again".

**Three new levels a day.** Past the free chapter, each day opens 3 new Arcade levels. Quitting a level and coming back to it doesn't cost another one.

**Short breaks.** Out of new levels? A 15-second break opens 3 more, up to 3 breaks a day. A break shows a hand-picked, kid-safe sponsor from our own ad server (a picture or a short muted video), or, when there isn't one, Luna with a chart fact. No ad network, no tracking, no identifiers and nothing to tap out to. Think of it as the pit stop in Mario Kart: quick, then back on the track.

**Moon Club Pass: $1.99 a year.** Every level in every climb, no breaks, with Family Sharing. It lives behind the grown-up gate (For grown-ups, then answer the multiplication question), with Restore and Manage right there. Like the One Ring, but only one of them is any fun to carry around.

## What to test
- **Free run:** Candle Academy levels 1–20, Line Lab 1–20 and Moon Tower 1–10 play with no counter.
- **Daily levels:** past those, the level card says how many new levels are left today. After 3 you get "That's today's new levels!"
- **Breaks:** "Take a short break" counts down 15 seconds, then "Collect 3 new levels" plays the level you picked. After 3 breaks, it's back tomorrow.
- **Pass:** "Ask a grown-up" opens the gate, then the Moon Club Pass sheet. In TestFlight the purchase is a free sandbox one; it should unlock everything right away. Then For grown-ups → Moon Club Pass shows it as on, and Restore works on a second device.

## Our promises
No accounts and no tracking, and everything stays on the device. Breaks never link out and never use an ad network. Pretend money in the games, always, and never advice. Hakuna matata.
