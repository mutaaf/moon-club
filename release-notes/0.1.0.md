## What to test
- **Luna's Lemonade Stand.** Shark Tank, but the sharks are in second grade. Run the stand, cut it into slices and find out what a *share* really is. No Krabby Patty secret formula required.
- **Save or Grow? and Your Slice Pays You.** Two more quest chapters. Choose wisely, Indiana Jones style: piggy bank or planting a money tree?
- **Moon School.** 28 bite-size lessons across 7 units. Wax on, wax off: tap, sort, check, level up.
- **Guess the Report Card.** Beat or miss? It's like a Pokémon battle, except the companies don't know they're in it.
- **Pretend Piggy Bank.** Real prices, Monopoly money. Nobody passes Go, nobody collects $200, nothing is real.
- **Family League.** Swap MOON- codes across the dinner table, like trading cards minus the playground drama. Codes also work with the website.
- **Ask Luna.** Curious questions welcome. She's more Baymax than HAL 9000.
- **The aurora sky.** Drawn on the GPU, and it naps like Snorlax when it's off screen or in Low Power Mode. Turn on Reduce Motion and it holds still.

## Grown-ups
- Tap the grown-ups corner and solve the quick math gate.
- Inside: what kids learn, conversation starters and the Family League.
- Optional: a friendlier voice using your own key. It's kept in the Keychain and only reads app text and Luna's answers, never anything a kid types.

## Our promises
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. What happens in Moon Club stays on the device.

Found a bug? Shake your phone or screenshot it in TestFlight. Every bug is just an Easter egg we haven't explained yet.
