# Moon Club 0.10.1: "Fill the Frame"

A tidy-up patch, Marie Kondo style: if a card had empty space that didn't spark joy, it got filled. Same Game On fun, with every screen packed edge to edge.

## Luna's news
- [rectangle.fill] Quiz cards are full now. Big picture up top, answers near your thumb.
- [crown.fill] Quiz bosses show up BIG. They flinch when you get one right!
- [arrow.left.arrow.right.circle.fill] Better pictures: a trade for the cow, a big building for the market.
- [map.fill] The map header shows the land you are on, and nothing hides under it.

## What to test
- **Lesson quiz steps.** On "check" and "sort" steps, the picture from what Luna just said now fills the top of the card, with Luna thinking it over. The question and the answers sit lower, where your thumb is. No more big blank space under the answers, on small phones or big ones.
- **Say steps.** The picture grows or shrinks to fit the card, so a short card still looks finished.
- **Quiz bosses.** The Captain's Quiz and the Island Quiz put the boss front and center, like the final round of a Kirby boss fight. A right answer makes it flinch and flash, and a miss gets a little "Ha!" The question card is dark like the stage and only as tall as it needs to be. The health bar stays at the top.
- **Pictures.** "A cow for some bread" now shows a trade (two arrows), not a paw print. The stock market is a big building, not a sports court. Tug-of-war days show two arrows pulling apart. Trading cards are a stack of cards. "Ups and downs" is a wiggly line, never an up arrow (it was showing an up arrow for "the trend goes down", which even Doc Brown would call a paradox).
- **The map.** The header is solid now, so stops and stars slide neatly under it instead of showing through. It also names the land you are on.
- **Quests.** A new check measures every quest stage on a small iPhone SE and an iPhone 17, so none of them need scrolling at regular text size.
