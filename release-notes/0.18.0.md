# Moon Club 0.18.0: "Moon Tower"

A whole new way to play: the Moon Tower. Tap the purple stairs button on the World map and start climbing. There are 100 levels from the Launch Pad up through the Cloud Deck, Sky Bridge, Orbit Ring and Moon Gate, then Deep Space, which never ends. The sky turns from day to dusk to a sky full of stars as you go up, like a slow-motion rocket launch in The Martian.

Every level is built from its number, so it's the same climb on every device. It gets harder as you go: more rounds, longer charts, four answers instead of three, look-alike answers (a big up day next to a small one), and from the Orbit Ring up, three stars need a perfect run. Every 10th level is a floor boss that mixes every game you've met, on the dark boss stage. Now and then a level has a twist: Long haul (three extra rounds), Four doors, Long view (longer charts) or Glass rocket (just one heart).

Three new games join the chart games, each borrowed from a classic:
- **Candle Pop**, like whack-a-mole: candles pop out of moon craters, tap the up days (higher up, sometimes the down days) before they sink. A wrong tap takes back a point, never a heart.
- **Memory flip**, like the card game: match each candle or trend line to its name.
- **Line them up**, like sorting a hand of cards: tap the days from the biggest up day to the biggest down day.

There are no clocks that cost hearts. Running out of hearts still just means "fuel up and try again", Relaxed play slows Candle Pop down, and the speed ring only ever gives bonus coins. Like a Jedi in training on Dagobah, the climb is about practice.

Also in this build: on the open iPhone Duo, the World map's page no longer has the title panel over it (from 0.17.5).

## What to test
- **Open it:** World tab → the purple stairs button (it shows your level). The tower should start on your level, with your rocket next to it.
- **Climb:** win level 1 and the next pad should open. Every 10th level is a boss with every game you've met. Tap a locked pad to see what it needs.
- **New games:** level 3 is Candle Pop, level 6 is Memory flip, level 24 is Line them up. Does Candle Pop feel too fast or too slow? (Relaxed play in the grown-up corner slows it down.)
- **Hard levels:** try one past level 60. Four answers, longer charts, and three stars only for a perfect run.
- **iPhone Duo, open:** the World map page should start at the top with nothing over it.
