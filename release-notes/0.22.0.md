# Moon Club 0.22.0: "The Moon Pass glow-up"

The daily level limit used to be a polite "come back later" note. Now it's more like Mario Kart's item box: you can see exactly what's in it.

**Today's moons.** Every Arcade level card shows today's new levels as little glowing moons that dim as you play. The limit sheet shows all twelve possible slots: the daily three, plus three more behind each break, with a lock on the ones still closed. It also counts down to midnight ("New levels in 5 h 12 min"), so nobody has to ask "are we there yet?"

**Breaks that teach.** When there's no sponsor, Luna's 15-second break is now a one-question chart quiz: a fact, a question, two big answers and the "why" either way. It works like a Jeopardy round where a wrong answer still gets you a lesson. Then "Collect" pops three gold moons up the screen with confetti.

**For grown-ups.** The Moon Club Pass offer has a Free vs Pass table. Ask to Buy is handled: the purchase waits for a family organizer, then switches on by itself. When the Pass turns on there's a thank-you screen with fireworks. The grown-up corner shows when the current Pass year ends, today's moons and breaks, and a clear answer after Restore purchases. Like Doc Brown's DeLorean, the daily ledger can't be time-traveled: winding the device clock back no longer hands out a fresh day, and setting it far ahead doesn't lock the real days after.

Also included: the Arcade on the home screen and the open-Duo freeze fix from 0.21.0.

## What to test
- **Moons:** open a Candle Academy level past level 20. Under Play you'll see glowing moons plus "n new levels left today", and they dim as you play. The Moon Arcade header on home shows them too.
- **Limit:** after the daily three, the sheet shows the Daily / Break 1–3 moons, the midnight countdown, the break button (with breaks left), Replay and Ask a grown-up.
- **Quiz break:** take a break with no sponsor. Answer the question, read the why, wait out the ring, tap Collect, and the level starts.
- **Pass (sandbox):** grown-up corner → Moon Club Pass. Check the Free vs Pass table, subscribe with a sandbox account, see the thank-you screen, then Restore purchases and its message.
