# Moon Club 0.16.2: "Living Lessons"

The lesson cards used to be little colored squares with one icon. Now every card is a tiny window into the northern lights, and Luna is inside acting out the lesson. Think of it as the Hogwarts portraits, but they teach money.

## Luna's news
- [sparkles] Every lesson card is a little show now. Watch me act it out!
- [play.circle.fill] Up next is big and bright, and I tell you what it's about.

## What to test
- **Moon School list.** Each card shows an aurora in its unit's colors with Luna and a prop: coins dropping into a piggy, a lemonade cup filling, candles growing, a magnifier sweeping a report card. Do the kids know what a lesson is about before they read the title?
- **Up next.** The top card is a full aurora with a bigger Luna and a speech bubble. Is the white title easy to read?
- **Open a lesson.** The first step shows the same scene, big, so tapping feels like zooming in. Like stepping through the wardrobe into Narnia.
- **Done lessons.** Calm and still, Luna smiling, a green check.
- **Battery and smoothness.** Cards move for a few seconds, then settle. They pause while you scroll and stop in Low Power Mode or with Reduce Motion on. On an iPad, does the list still scroll smoothly? If anything stutters, tell us.

## Our promises
Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and everything stays on the device. Pretend money only, never advice. Small cards, big show; as Ratatouille taught us, anyone can learn.
