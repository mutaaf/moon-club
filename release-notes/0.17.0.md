# Moon Club 0.17.0: "Super Suit"

Moon Club put on a cape. Since the last App Store version (0.15.0) it learned to point at one next step, turned every lesson card into a tiny stage show, and opens like a storybook on iPhone Duo. The update news got a new look too: a headline, a few bright cards and one big button. Like a shooting star, it flies by once and gets out of the way.

## Luna's news

## What to test
- **Fresh look: the update card.** After an update, Luna now waves hello with a headline and up to four bright cards, then one big "Let's go" button. It shows once per version, never on a first install, and never again once seen. No links, nothing to type. The full notes stay under For grown-ups → What's new. Ready, set, launch: update from 0.15.0 and see it fly by.
- **Story: one big button (0.16.1).** Luna says hi above ONE glowing button with the next step and how long it takes. Other missions wait in a small list underneath. Can you tell what to tap in two seconds?
- **Moon School: living lesson cards (0.16.2).** Each card is a little window of northern lights with Luna acting out the lesson: coins drop into a piggy, a lemonade cup fills, candles grow. Cards move for a few seconds, then rest. They stay still with Reduce Motion or Low Power Mode on.
- **For grown-ups (0.16.0).** Bright "At a glance" tiles, a How to play section, and long sections that fold away until you tap them.
- **World map on iPad (0.16.0).** The sea stays calm until you scroll, so the map opens smoothly.
- **iPhone Duo (0.16.3, 0.16.4).** Opened, every tab reads like a two-page book with nothing in the fold. Closed, buttons stay clear of the camera and on screen, and turned sideways, lessons shrink the picture so the words and answers fit.

## Our promises
Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and everything stays on the device. Pretend money only, never advice. Every superhero needs a sidekick, and here it's the grown-up with the lock button.
