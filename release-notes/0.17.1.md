# Moon Club 0.17.1: "Unstuck"

With an iPhone Duo open, starting a lesson or game could freeze Moon Club until it closed. Like Frozen's Anna with a thaw, we've worked to melt that freeze. Please try it and tell us.

## Luna's news
> Moon Club got a super suit!
- [hand.point.up.left.fill] **Super button!** Not sure what's next? Tap it. I pick for you!
- [sparkles] **Lights, camera, lessons!** Every lesson card is a little show. I act it out!
- [moon.stars.fill] **Shooting star news** After each update, I pop in to show what's new.
- [book.pages.fill] **Open like a storybook** Open your iPhone Duo. Each side has its own job!

## What to test
- **iPhone Duo open, start anything.** Open a lesson, a game, a quest and a boss from Story, World and Guess. Each should open right away and keep moving.
- **If it still freezes:** wait for it to close, then tap **Share** when TestFlight asks about the crash. That sends us the exact spot it got stuck, so we can find it fast.
- **Closed Duo, iPhone, iPad.** Should look and play like 0.17.0.

## Our promises
Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and everything stays on the device. Pretend money only, never advice. Like the Tin Man with his oil can, a little care and everything moves again.
