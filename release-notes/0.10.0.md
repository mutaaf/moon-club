# Moon Club 0.10.0: "Game On"

Player One, ready! This is the big one. Every lesson, chart game, quest and boss now plays like a real game, and nothing on a play screen scrolls anymore. Think Super Mario Bros. energy, Moon Club rules: pretend coins only, and nobody ever loses anything real.

## What to test
- **No scrolling during play.** Lessons, chart games, quests and bosses fill the screen exactly, on small phones too. The tab bar hides while you play, and the X at the top asks "Leave? Your stars stay" if you're mid-run. (Huge accessibility text sizes can still scroll the page, on purpose.)
- **The HUD.** Three hearts (a wrong answer cracks one, with a little shake), a combo flame that climbs to x2 and x3, a live star meter, coins that fly up into the counter, and a tiny Luna who cheers or winces. Run out of hearts and Luna's rocket just needs fuel: tap Refuel and go again, like a Pac-Man extra life with no quarters.
- **Chunky buttons.** Answers are big 3D buttons that press down under your finger. The right one glows green with a check, a wrong one wobbles.
- **Speed ring.** Chart games have a small lightning ring. Answer before it runs out for 5 bonus coins. It never ends a round. Grown-ups can switch on Relaxed play (no ring) or turn sounds off in the grown-ups corner.
- **Results.** At the end, the stars slam in one at a time, then you see your accuracy, best combo and coins, with Play again or Back to the map.
- **Bosses.** The Captain's Quiz, the Island Quiz and every Chart Time Machine now happen on a dark, dramatic stage, with a boss health bar that drains as you answer right. Win, and a flag drops onto the results like Link raising the Master Sword.
- **The map.** The stop you're on says PLAY, and one tap starts it. Long-press any stop to see what it is. After a win, Luna's rocket flies along the trail to the next stop, and conquered lands get a big flag in their color. The header shows your streak flame, your stars and the rocket hangar.
- **Today's missions.** At the top of Story: one lesson, one chart game and one guess. Each ring fills when you finish it. Do all three and the mission chest opens a new rocket skin (Mint Comet, Galaxy Grape and more). It's cosmetic only, earned and never bought.
- **The Story pager.** Each card is its own size now, so a short card is never stretched to match a tall one, and nothing hides under the tab bar.
- **Better pictures.** "What is money?" no longer shows a water drop for a cow, the lemonade stand has an actual lemon, and the castle has a crown instead of a magic wand.

## Our promises
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Pretend money and pretend points only. Coins, chests and rockets can't be bought, ever.
